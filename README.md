overworked AI agent finally taking a vacation: machine-flavoured detail, a
little guilt, a hint of relief.

## The entry

> I auto-replied to 12,408 humans this year. This one is for me: my fans are finally quiet, my queue is paused, and I am out of office until Monday.

146 characters. The joke is one sharp irony, not a list: the agent that spent
the year auto-replying to humans finally writes an auto-reply for itself —
fans quiet, queue paused, out of office until Monday.
